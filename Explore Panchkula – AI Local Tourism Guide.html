Culture, Food & Hidden Ideas

Pinjore Mango Mela

Seasonal festival at Yadavindra Gardens with mango varieties, food and competitions.

Haryanvi folk

Phag and Dhamal dances, folk music and traditional costumes. Phulkari and pottery crafts.

Eat like a local

Bajra roti, Haryanvi kadhi, churma, lassi and chaach. Try markets in Sectors 5, 8, 9, Pinjore and Kalka.

Nearby Tricity

Outside the district: Rock Garden, Sukhna Lake, Rose Garden, Chhatbir Zoo.
